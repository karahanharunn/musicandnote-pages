Privacy Policy

Chat Analyzer never uploads your chat.

Effective date: 2026-09-22. Chat Analyzer reads a WhatsApp chat export you choose, analyzes it on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence, and keeps the messages, the names you enter and every verdict on your device.

Back to Chat Analyzer legal Back to legal home

What Chat Analyzer stores on your device

  • The chat export you import, parsed into messages, until you delete the analysis.
  • The people, situation and question you enter, and the verdict the app produces.
  • Your saved analyses (the "record") and custom scenarios.

No account, no backend

There is no sign-in and no server of ours. The chat text is processed by Apple Intelligence on the device and is not sent to us or to any third-party AI service.

Files access

The app only reads the file you pick in the Files picker or share to it from WhatsApp. It does not browse your files, photos or contacts.

Analytics and logging

The app uses PostHog, a third-party product analytics service, to understand how it is used. We send a small set of product events — app launches, screen views, onboarding steps, analyses started and completed, import and analysis errors by type, review prompts — together with the app version and an anonymous device identifier. We never send the chat, the names you enter, your question, or the verdict text. PostHog processes the data on our behalf on servers in the United States. We do not use this data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Debug file logging is limited to debug builds.

Deleting your data

Delete an analysis from the record, or delete the app: everything it stored goes with it.
